# SafeGuard-AI
An AI safety and legal-tech framework for advanced weapons monitoring.
## Inspiration
With the rapid advancement of autonomous AI, the risk of integrating artificial intelligence into high stakes military and weapon systems such as nuclear arsenals has become a critical global threat. We were inspired to build SafeGuard AI to bridge the gap between emerging AI safety protocols and international humanitarian law.

## What it does
SafeGuard AI is an AI powered legal and technical compliance layer designed to monitor, analyze, and automatically block unauthorized or hazardous autonomous commands on advanced weapons systems. It enforces international arms control laws in real-time.

## How we built it
We utilized a rule based AI architecture combined with legal text analysis parsers to evaluate incoming autonomous directives against pre established safety frameworks and international treaties.

## Challenges we ran into
Balancing strict automated safety blocks with complex operational legal exceptions while maintaining a rapid response threshold.

## What we learned
We gained deeper insights into the intersection of legal tech, international policy, and the critical importance of human in the-loop AI safety mechanisms.
